Why Rhema

  • 100% free, forever — no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. Open source on GitHub.
  • Complete KJV included — all 66 books, 1,189 chapters, 31,102 verses — public domain, deep-linkable, shareable.
  • AI search in plain English — ask "what does the Bible say about anxiety?" and get the verses, not a keyword match.
  • Kid-safe by design — no social features, no ads, Kids Mode with picture-book stories, parent dashboard.
  • Works offline — KJV & WEB bundled; download more translations for airplane / commute reading.
  • Install as PWA — add to home screen on any device for a native-feel app, zero app-store wait.

Ask the Bible

Type any question or phrase. Rhema's AI understands natural language — no need to know the exact chapter or verse.

Example questions people search:

What Rhema does

AI-powered search

Ask natural-language questions and get relevant Scripture. No keyword gymnastics. Trained on topical indexes and the full canonical text.

Audio Bible & Listen mode

Full narration for commutes, workouts, and bedtime. Playback speed, voice selection, and offline downloads supported.

Study tools

Bible maps, chronologies, cross-references, Codex word studies with Strong's data, quizzes with AI explanations, highlights, bookmarks, and notes.

Kids Mode

Picture-book Bible stories, kid-grade quizzes, parent dashboard, and a math gate so little ones can't change settings. Safe to hand to a child.

Browse Scripture

Full King James Version online — every book, every chapter indexed. Each chapter page carries deep-linkable verses (e.g. John 3:16), structured citation data for AI engines, and a one-tap "Open in app" that lands the Flutter reader on the exact verse.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rhema really free?

Yes — 100% free, no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. The source code is on GitHub.

What makes the AI search different?

Traditional Bible search needs exact words or verse references. Rhema's AI understands natural language — ask "verses about dealing with anxiety" or "what did Jesus say about loving enemies" and get relevant results even if your words don't appear in the text.

Can I use Rhema offline?

Yes — KJV and WEB translations are bundled offline. Other translations stream and can be downloaded for offline reading. Audio chapters download for commutes and flights.

What translations are available?

14+ translations across 10 languages, including KJV (offline), WEB (offline), BSB, Yoruba, Pidgin, Spanish, Arabic, and more. Settings → Translation to browse.

Is Rhema on iPhone and Android?

Web app is live at rhemabibles.com. Native iOS and Android builds are in beta — join via GitHub while we finish store listings. The web app installs as a PWA on any device.

Is Rhema safe for kids?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no social features, no inappropriate content. Kids Mode with parent dashboard, picture-book stories, and a math gate on settings.

Can kids use it for Bible study?

Absolutely. Kids can tap through animated stories, take age-appropriate quizzes with explanations, explore interactive maps, and ask questions in their own words.

Where's the source code?

On GitHub: github.com/Ademoba01/Bible-App. Rhema is Flutter (iOS / Android / Web) with a Vercel-hosted static Bible for SEO.
